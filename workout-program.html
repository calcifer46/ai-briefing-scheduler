How the flexibility works

  • No fixed calendar — just work through Day A → B → C → A in order, whenever you actually get to the gym.
  • 2 sessions this week? You did A and B — pick up on C next time.
  • 4 sessions? A, B, C, then straight back to A.
  • Every exercise is editable (name, sets, reps, rest) — tap any field to change it. Use the trash icon to remove a row, or "+ Add exercise" to insert one.
  • Rep ranges are 6-12 for hypertrophy. Once you hit the top of the range for all sets, add weight next session (progressive overload).
